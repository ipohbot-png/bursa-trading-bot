A dip-buying swing rule for TMK & Powerwell

116 technical rule combinations across 8 indicator families were tested on Powerwell (0217, ACE) in-sample 2020–2023, then confirmed out-of-sample 2024–now and on TMK Chemical's (5330, Main) full listed history — every trade filled at next-day open, board-lot sized at RM 20,000, and netted through the full Maybank fee schedule. Two near-identical rules survived. This page shows them, their exact entry/exit conditions, and what they honestly do and don't deliver.

The promoted rule — RSI(2) pullback in an uptrend
Enter long when (both true at close)
  • Close is above the 50-day SMA (uptrend intact)
  • RSI(2) drops below 25 (sharp short-term dip)

Buy at the next session's open.

Exit at next open when (first to occur)
  • RSI(2) rises above 75 (dip has bounced), or
  • 15 trading days elapse (10-day variant equally valid), or
  • Close falls 5% below your entry price (stop-loss)

Typical hold 4–6 sessions · in the market only ~14–18% of the time · signals fire ~9×/year on Powerwell, ~7×/year on TMK.

Read this before trading it. In the 2024–2026 rally this rule made +28% on Powerwell while simply holding made +285%. Its value is not beating a strong uptrend — it can't, it's only invested ~15% of the time. Its value is disciplined, mechanical entries with a hard stop, small drawdowns, and cash in hand ~85% of the time. If you believe the uptrend continues, holding beats this rule; if you want rule-based dip entries with controlled risk, this is the only pattern out of 116 that survived testing honestly.

How the result was validated

  • protocolParameters tuned only on Powerwell 2020–2023; 2024+ and all of TMK held out. One shared rule set — no per-stock tuning.
  • executionSignals at close, fills at next session's open; orders carry past zero-volume bars; positions sized to board lots within available cash — no margin, no same-bar fills, no intraday stops (daily data can't model them; the 5% stop triggers on close, exits next open, so real slippage past −5% is possible).
  • feesEvery leg charged the full Maybank 2026 schedule (0.10% brokerage min RM8 + SST, 0.03% clearing, RM1.50/RM1000 stamp). A RM 20k round trip costs ≈ RM 115 (0.58%).
  • verificationThree independent passes: the engine's 15-test suite; an adversarial code review that caught and fixed a margin-sizing bug (which had been overstating returns ~12 points — results here are post-fix); and an independent re-derivation of all 131 promoted-rule trades from raw prices, which matched exactly.

Honest limitations

  • TMK has only ~19 months of trading history (listed Dec 2024) — 11 trades is thin evidence. Treat TMK results as "consistent with", not "proof of".
  • The edge is modest: ~+1% per trade on ~9 trades a year is roughly +9%/yr while invested only ~15% of the time. On RM 20k that's ~RM 1,800/yr before slippage.
  • Data is yfinance daily bars — good enough for research, not broker-grade. Spreads on these names (2–3 sen on a ~RM 0.80 stock) can eat a meaningful slice of a 1% edge; use limit orders.
  • Past ≠ future. Both stocks are in strong uptrends; the rule's uptrend filter means it stops trading entirely if price falls below the 50-day SMA — by design.
